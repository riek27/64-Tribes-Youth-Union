Our Purpose

Why 64 Tribes Youth Union Exists

We believe that young people are the key agents of change. By uniting youth from all 64 tribes of South Sudan, we create a platform for peacebuilding, leadership development, and community transformation. Through dialogue, volunteerism, and advocacy, we are building a more inclusive and peaceful future.

Who We Are

United for Peace & Unity

64 Tribes Youth Union is a youth-led non-profit organization based in Juba, South Sudan. We bring together young people from all 64 tribes to foster peace, develop leadership, and drive community development through inclusive programs and advocacy.

Mission

Empower youth through peacebuilding, leadership, and community engagement.

Vision

A peaceful, united, and prosperous South Sudan driven by youth.

Values

Unity, Peace, Leadership, Inclusiveness, Integrity, Diversity.

Focus Areas

Our Programs

Peacebuilding

Promoting peaceful coexistence, dialogue, reconciliation, and conflict resolution among communities.

Youth Leadership

Developing future leaders through training, mentorship, and hands-on leadership opportunities.

Community Development

Supporting initiatives that improve education, health, livelihoods, and local communities.

Civic Engagement

Encouraging active youth participation in governance, democracy, and nation-building.

Advocacy

Advocating for youth rights, inclusion, gender equality, and equal opportunities.

Capacity Building

Providing workshops, training, and skills development programs for young people.
